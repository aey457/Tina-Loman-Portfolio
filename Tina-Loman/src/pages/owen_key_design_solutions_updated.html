05 / Key Design Solutions

Designing for Clarity and Collaboration

Designing for complex government workflows required balancing usability, accessibility, and operational needs. Across both client-facing portals, I focused on simplifying digital submissions, supporting structured data collection, and enabling clearer collaboration between municipalities and provincial ministries.

01

Solution 01 — Streamlined Digital Submissions

AddressesManual ProcessesLack of Online Services

Structured application flows, save-and-resume functionality, and clearer case tracking for municipal users.

The Need

Municipal users needed a more intuitive way to complete complex planning submissions and manage cases without relying on fragmented manual processes.

My Design Approach

Designed guided submission workflows that integrated optional pre-consultation, with clear navigation, contextual instructions, status indicators, and save-and-resume functionality to support complex, multi-step applications.

Intended Benefit

Make submissions easier to complete, reduce administrative friction, and improve visibility into case progress.

Solution 02 — Smart Forms & Structured Data Collection

AddressesData InconsistencyLimited Reporting

Designed interactive, data-driven forms to support consistent information capture and future reporting.

The Need

Manual data entry and fragmented tracking in the legacy system created inconsistencies and limited reporting capabilities.

My Design Approach

Designed interactive application and commenting forms with contextual guidance, validation feedback, and database-driven selections. Adapted experimental Ontario Design System components and prototyped interactions in Figma, collaborating with cross-functional teams to refine interface messaging.

Intended Benefit

Improve data entry consistency, reduce manual reconciliation, and support structured data collection for future reporting and analytics.

Solution 03 — Cross-ministry Review & Collaboration

AddressesEmail-based CirculationFragmented Workflows

Structured case circulation, review, and comment submission workflows to support coordination across provincial ministries.

The Need

Partner ministries needed a clearer way to review planning cases, coordinate responsibilities, and provide feedback across organizations.

My Design Approach

Designed case review and comment submission interfaces with structured information, contextual actions, and role-aware workflows to support collaboration.

Intended Benefit

Improve clarity around review responsibilities, support more consistent feedback, and reduce communication friction across ministries.
